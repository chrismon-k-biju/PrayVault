Divine Mercy Chaplet

Karuna Kontha

A beautiful prayer of trust and petition, calling down God’s abundant mercy on our families, campus, and the entire world.

1. Sign of the Cross
In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
2. Opening Prayers (Optional)
You expired, Jesus, but the source of life gushed forth for souls, and the ocean of mercy opened up for the whole world. O Fount of Life, unfathomable Divine Mercy, envelop the whole world and empty Yourself out upon us.
O Blood and Water, which gushed forth from the Heart of Jesus as a fount of Mercy for us, I trust in You! (3 times)
3. The Foundation Prayers
Our Father: Our Father, Who art in heaven, hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

Hail Mary: Hail Mary, full of grace. The Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

Apostles' Creed: I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, and in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died and was buried; He descended into hell; on the third day He rose again from the dead; He ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there He will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.
4. On the Decades (On the Rosary Beads)
On the large bead before each decade:
"Eternal Father, I offer you the Body and Blood, Soul and Divinity of Your Dearly Beloved Son, Our Lord, Jesus Christ, in atonement for our sins and those of the whole world."
On the ten small beads of each decade:
"For the sake of His sorrowful Passion, have mercy on us and on the whole world."
5. Closing Prayers (Repeat 3 times)
"Holy God, Holy Mighty One, Holy Immortal One, have mercy on us and on the whole world."
6. Final Prayer (Optional)
Eternal God, in whom mercy is endless and the treasury of compassion inexhaustible, look kindly upon us and increase Your mercy in us, that in difficult moments we might not despair nor become despondent, but with great confidence submit ourselves to Your holy will, which is Love and Mercy itself. Amen.

The Mysteries of the Rosary

Click on each mystery group to view the meditations for each day of the week.

The Joyful Mysteries (Monday & Saturday)
1. The Annunciation
The Angel Gabriel announces to Mary that she will conceive the Son of God. (Humility)
2. The Visitation
Mary visits her cousin Elizabeth who is pregnant with John the Baptist. (Love of Neighbor)
3. The Nativity
Jesus is born in a stable in Bethlehem. (Poverty of Spirit)
4. The Presentation
Mary and Joseph present baby Jesus in the Temple. (Obedience to God's Will)
5. The Finding in the Temple
After being lost for three days, Jesus is found teaching the elders in the Temple. (Joy in finding Jesus)
The Luminous Mysteries (Thursday)
1. The Baptism in the Jordan
Jesus is baptized by John and declared the beloved Son of God. (Gratitude for Faith)
2. The Wedding at Cana
Jesus performs His first public miracle by changing water into wine. (To Jesus through Mary)
3. The Proclamation of the Kingdom
Jesus calls all people to repentance and proclaims the Good News. (Repentance and Trust)
4. The Transfiguration
Jesus is transfigured in glory before Peter, James, and John. (Desire for Holiness)
5. The Institution of the Eucharist
At the Last Supper, Jesus gives us His Body and Blood. (Eucharistic Devotion)
The Sorrowful Mysteries (Tuesday & Friday)
1. The Agony in the Garden
Jesus sweats blood as He prays in Gethsemane on the eve of His passion. (Sorrow for Sin)
2. The Scourging at the Pillar
Jesus is bound and cruelly whipped by Roman soldiers. (Purity and Mortification)
3. The Crowning with Thorns
A crown of sharp thorns is pressed into Jesus' head. (Moral Courage)
4. The Carrying of the Cross
Jesus carries the heavy wooden cross to Calvary. (Patience in Trials)
5. The Crucifixion
Jesus is nailed to the cross, dies after three hours of agony. (Forgiveness and Self-Sacrifice)
The Glorious Mysteries (Wednesday & Sunday)
1. The Resurrection
Jesus rises glorious from the dead on the third day. (Faith)
2. The Ascension
Jesus ascends into heaven forty days after His resurrection. (Hope)
3. The Descent of the Holy Spirit
The Holy Spirit descends upon Mary and the Apostles in tongues of fire. (Love and Wisdom)
4. The Assumption
At the end of her life, Mother Mary is taken body and soul into heaven. (Union with Mary)
5. The Coronation
Mother Mary is crowned Queen of Heaven and Earth. (Perseverance in Grace)
Daily Reflections

Daily Prayers

Simple prayers to structure your morning, evening, and moments of study during the busy college week.

Morning Offering

O Jesus, through the Immaculate Heart of Mary, I offer You my prayers, works, joys, and sufferings of this day for all the intentions of Your Sacred Heart, in union with the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass throughout the world, for the salvation of souls, the reparation of sins, the reunion of all Christians, and in particular for the growth and holiness of our AJCE campus fellowship. Amen.

Prayer to the Holy Spirit

Come, Holy Spirit, fill the hearts of Your faithful and kindle in them the fire of Your love. Send forth Your Spirit and they shall be created, and You shall renew the face of the earth. O God, who by the light of the Holy Spirit did instruct the hearts of the faithful, grant that by the same Holy Spirit we may be truly wise and ever rejoice in His consolations, through Christ our Lord. Amen.

Prayer Before Study

O Creator, who out of the treasures of Your wisdom, have appointed hierarchies of angels and set them in wonderful order, pour down a ray of Your brightness upon my intellect. Dispel the double darkness of sin and ignorance in which I was born. Grant me sharpness of understanding, capacity to retain, and method and ease in learning. Guide the beginning, direct the progress, and crown the completion of my studies. Amen. (St. Thomas Aquinas)
